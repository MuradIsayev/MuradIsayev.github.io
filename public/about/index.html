About me

Hey, my name is Murad Isayev, and this page is all about me.

I’m a Master’s student in Global Software Development at Fulda University . I moved to Germany in October 2024, and I work at a bookstore to support myself. Technically speaking, Go is my primary programming language right now for learning and building. In the past, I have worked extensively with Javascript/TypeScript (Node.js ) with the focus on both backend and frontend. I have also written some Java and C from project to project. Other than that, I use NeoVim (proud face) as my text editor.

Work Experience

Full Stack Developer @ iLoyal

May 2023 – Oct 2024  •  Tech: Node.js NestJS React MySQL Redis

Following our successful launch across Azerbaijan, the platform expanded into the German market, bringing smart table management to partners like Pano Ravensburg and Cafe Docia.
